Privacy Policy — WiFi Scanner

Last updated: 25 November 2026

WiFi Scanner ("the App") is a free Android utility for checking your own WiFi network: scanning nearby networks, measuring signal strength, finding the best channel, testing internet speed, listing devices connected to your WiFi, building a home signal map, and scanning or generating WiFi QR codes.

Information we collect

We collect nothing. The App has no account, no sign-in, and no registration. It does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information, and it contains no advertising and no analytics.

Information stored on your device only

The following stays only on your own phone and is never sent anywhere:

  • Your saved signal-map rooms and their measured signal strength.
  • Your saved speed-test history.
  • The list of device addresses (MAC addresses) the App has seen on your WiFi, used only to flag a newly connected device.

You can delete the signal map and the speed history from inside the App at any time.

Permissions and how they are used

  • Location: Android requires location permission to list nearby WiFi networks. The App uses it only on your device to show those networks. Your location is never recorded or sent anywhere.
  • Nearby WiFi devices: used only to scan for nearby WiFi networks on your device.
  • Camera: used only to scan a WiFi QR code you point the camera at. Photos are never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Internet: used only for the speed test (which connects to Cloudflare's public speed-test service) and for a short ping to check the connection. No personal data is sent with these checks.

Third parties

The App sends no data to us or to any third party, except the speed-test connection described above, which carries no personal information.

Children

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
